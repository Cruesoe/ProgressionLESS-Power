# ProgressionLESS: Power

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Overhauls power research, so every generator, battery and power building has a place in the research tree.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Power adds up to 22 research projects depending on your mods. This fork groups them into at most 6, such as fueled generators, wind power and turbines, and puts VFE Power's advanced generators back on its own advanced power sources project. Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.
